Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about learning with KPX Academy.

What is KPX Academy?+
KPX Academy is a practical skills development, creative learning, and entrepreneurial platform dedicated to helping individuals acquire useful skills, explore their creativity, and discover new possibilities. We provide practical training, knowledge-based programmes, and creative workshops designed to support personal and professional development.
Who can enrol in your training programmes?+
Our programmes are open to individuals interested in learning practical skills or expanding their knowledge. This includes students, graduates, working professionals, business owners, stay-at-home mums, job seekers, and aspiring entrepreneurs. Whether you want to acquire a new skill, develop an existing one, explore a creative interest, create an additional source of income or expand your business opportunities, our programmes provide opportunities to learn, grow and put useful knowledge into practice.
Do I need previous experience before enrolling?+
No. Many of our programmes are suitable for beginners, so you do not necessarily need previous experience. Each programme is structured to introduce the relevant concepts and guide you through the learning process. Where prior knowledge or specific requirements are necessary, they will be communicated before enrolment.
Are your training programmes online or physical?+
We offer both online and physical training, depending on the programme. Online programmes are delivered through Telegram, while selected programmes also have physical training options. Live one-on-one sessions are available for selected programmes and are arranged by appointment.
How do online training and live classes work?+
All our online training programmes are conducted through Telegram, where lessons are delivered through recorded practical video demonstrations, e-books, and other relevant instructional materials. This allows you to access the lessons, learn at your own pace, and revisit the materials whenever necessary. Our live classes, on the other hand, are conducted through direct, one-on-one video sessions with the instructor. The training date, time, and session schedule are arranged in advance based on mutual availability and discussed with the learner before the class begins. This provides an opportunity for direct interaction, guided demonstrations, and practical learning during the session.
How do I access the training materials after registration?+
After successful registration and payment confirmation, you will receive the relevant access details and instructions. Depending on the programme, your materials may include practical videos, e-books, guides, recipes, and other supporting resources shared through the designated learning platform.
Will I be taught practical demonstrations?+
Yes. For hands-on programmes, practical demonstrations are included to help you understand the processes involved. You will be guided through relevant techniques, materials, and procedures so you can follow the demonstrations and practise what you learn.
Do I need to purchase materials or equipment before starting?+
This depends on the programme you choose. Some training programmes require specific materials or equipment for practical activities. Where necessary, the recommended materials and relevant purchasing information will be communicated to help you prepare.
Can I ask questions when I encounter difficulties during training?+
Yes. Mentorship and learner support are included as part of the training. You can ask questions, seek clarification, and receive guidance through the designated training community or support channel whenever you need assistance. Details on how to access the support channel will be provided after enrolment.
Do you provide supplier contacts and business guidance?+
Some programmes include supplier contacts, sourcing information, and business guidance to support your learning. The specific resources available depend on the programme you enrol in and will be outlined in its training details.
How can I register for a training programme?+
You can register through the relevant programme page on our website. Follow the registration instructions provided and ensure that you supply the required information correctly.
Can I register for more than one programme?+
Yes. You can enrol in multiple programmes based on your interests, budget, and the availability of each training option.
Can I make enquiries before making payment?+
Yes. We encourage prospective learners to make enquiries whenever they need clarification about a programme, its contents, fees, training format, or requirements. This helps you understand what you are registering for before making a payment.
What happens after I make payment?+
After making payment, you should follow the payment confirmation instructions provided during registration. Once your payment has been verified, you will receive the relevant training access details and instructions for getting started.
How do I choose the right programme for me?+
Consider your interests, the skills you would like to acquire, and what you hope to achieve from the training. You can review the available programme descriptions to understand what each one covers. If you need further clarification, you can contact us before making a registration decision.
Will I receive a certificate after completing my training?+
Learners who successfully complete an eligible programme and meet its applicable completion and qualification requirements may receive a certificate. Payment or access to a programme does not automatically qualify a learner for certification.
Are all programmes eligible for certificates?+
No. Certificates are available for selected practical skills training programmes. Certificate eligibility depends on the nature and requirements of each programme. The relevant certificate information is provided in the programme details.
What does the certificate represent?+
A certificate issued by KPX Academy recognises successful completion of an eligible training programme in accordance with its requirements. It acknowledges the learner's participation and achievement within the scope of the programme. It should not be interpreted as a government-issued professional licence or a guarantee of employment or business success.
Can my certificate be verified?+
Yes. Certificates issued by KPX Academy can be verified through the certificate verification system on our official website. This allows the authenticity of an issued certificate to be checked using its relevant verification details.
Can I start my own business after completing the training?+
Yes. The knowledge and practical skills you acquire can serve as a foundation for developing your own business. However, running a business also involves planning, product development, pricing, marketing, and customer relations. We will guide you through these important areas as part of your training. Your ability to apply the knowledge, remain consistent, and make informed business decisions will also play an important role in how you develop your business.
Do you guarantee that I will make money after the training?+
No. While our programmes provide practical knowledge, skills, and relevant business guidance, we do not guarantee a particular income or business outcome. Your results will depend on several factors, including how well you understand and apply what you learn, your consistency, planning, effort, and the decisions you make.
Can I use the skills I acquire for personal projects rather than business?+
Absolutely. The skills you acquire can be applied to personal projects, hobbies, gifts, creative activities, or other interests. You can also explore business opportunities if that is something you wish to pursue.
Do you organise physical workshops for schools and organisations?+
Yes. We organise creative and practical workshops for schools, organisations, groups, and other interested institutions. These workshops provide participants with opportunities to explore creative activities and learn practical skills in a guided setting.
Can an organisation request a private or customised workshop?+
Yes. Organisations, schools, and groups can make enquiries about private or customised workshops. The arrangement can be discussed based on the preferred activity, number of participants, event date, venue, and other requirements.
Do you produce souvenirs and customised gifts?+
Yes. We offer souvenirs and customised gift items for individuals, events, organisations, and special occasions. Available items may include resin keyholders, customised pens, jotters, bookmarks, tags, and other creative pieces.
Can I request customised colours, designs, or branding for souvenirs?+
Yes. Customisation requests can be discussed based on the type of item, preferred colours, design, quantity, and other specifications. Available options will depend on the product and production requirements.
Is there a minimum order quantity for souvenirs?+
Yes. Minimum order quantities may apply depending on the selected items and package. For our souvenir packages, the minimum order quantity is generally 20 pieces. You can contact us to discuss your specific requirements and receive further details.
Will you introduce more programmes in the future?+
Yes. We may introduce additional training programmes, workshops, and creative services as we continue to develop the platform. Updates about new offerings will be shared through our official communication channels and website.
What can I expect from learning with KPX Academy?+
You can expect a focus on practical knowledge, detailed training, and relevant learning resources. Our aim is to make useful skills and knowledge accessible while providing guidance that helps learners understand the skills they choose to explore. We encourage commitment, consistent practice, and the willingness to keep improving.
How can I get further assistance or make additional enquiries?+
If you have further questions or need clarification about any of our training programmes, workshops, or services, you can reach out to our team through the available contact channels. We will be happy to assist you with the information you need.
