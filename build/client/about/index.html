Why we exist

Learning should lead to something real.

A skill becomes more powerful when you understand how it works, have room to practise, and can see how it might fit into your life.

Clear instruction. Useful practice. More possibilities.

KPX Academy grew from experience across entrepreneurship, resin art and crafts, beauty and lip care, importation, and other creative ventures. Those experiences reinforced a simple lesson: knowing about a skill is only the beginning.

Understanding the process, practising properly, and knowing how to apply what you learn can make the difference between information you forget and knowledge you can build on.

We bring practical, skill-based training together in one place for people who want to explore an interest, develop an existing talent, or take a step towards a business idea.

Our aim is straightforward: make learning practical, worthwhile, and full of possibility.

Meet the founder

Built from experience. Shared with purpose.

I am Kepemi Oluwafunke, known as Keplex, an entrepreneur and the founder of KPX Academy.

My journey has taken me through different areas of entrepreneurship, including resin art and crafts, beauty and lip care, importation, and other creative ventures. Each experience has shaped how I approach learning, creating, and building a business.

I believe people deserve to understand what they are paying to learn. Detailed instruction, practical application, and the opportunity to ask questions and keep improving should be part of a worthwhile learning experience.

KPX Academy brings that belief into one place: a platform to share practical knowledge and help others take meaningful steps towards their own goals.

What guides us
