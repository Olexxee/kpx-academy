Custom Resin Pen

Custom-made resin pens designed to suit individual preferences and special occasions. Customers can choose their preferred colours, designs, and personalisation options to create unique pens for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, corporate gifts, souvenirs, and other special events. Personalisation may include names, dates, initials, and other customised inscriptions. Displayed samples are for illustration purposes only; customers can request colours and designs that match their preferred style or event theme.

Estimated production lead time: 4 days

Package terms

Minimum order quantity is 30 pieces. Customisation options include preferred colours, designs, and personalised inscriptions. Event themes and colour preferences must be specified to ensure the design complements the occasion. A design preview will be provided for approval before production begins. Production will commence only after approval of the final design. All names, spellings, dates, and inscriptions must be carefully checked and confirmed before design approval.
