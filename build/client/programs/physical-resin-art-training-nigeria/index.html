KPX – PHYSICAL RESIN ART & CRAFT TRAINING

Join our hands-on physical resin art and craft training in Ile-Ife, Osun State, Nigeria. Gain practical experience in resin mixing, colour application, moulding, decoration and the creation of different resin crafts, with guided instruction designed to help you develop your skills through direct practical sessions.

In-person training1 week

About this programme

Learn the fundamentals of resin art and develop practical skills in creating beautiful, customized resin art pieces for personal use, gifting, and business purposes. This hands-on training covers resin preparation, measuring ratios, color mixing, decoration techniques, pouring effects, and embedding techniques. You will learn to create a variety of resin crafts, including jewelry, souvenir keychains, customized writing pens, jotter packages, phone pouches, mirrors, combs, and hair clips. You will also gain practical business guidance on developing your resin art skills into a viable business. Upon successful completion of the training, you will receive a Certificate of Completion. You will also take home the resin art pieces you create during the practical sessions and enjoy lifetime mentorship access.

Learning outline

What the programme covers

Explore the topics included in this programme.

  1. 01

    Introduction to Resin Art (Introduction to resin art and materials for beginners)

  2. 02

    Resin Production Safety & Precautions (Safe resin handling, essential precautions, and proper working practices)

  3. 03

    Resin Measuring Ratios & Color Mixing (Understanding resin-to-hardener ratios, accurate measurements, and color mixing techniques)

  4. 04

    Resin Decoration & Coloring Techniques (Resin coloring, decorative effects, and creative finishing techniques)

  5. 05

    Resin Pouring & Design Techniques (Pouring methods and techniques for achieving different resin designs and effects)

  6. 06

    Resin Embedding Techniques (Embedding lights, flowers, glitters, photographs, names, and other decorative elements)

  7. 07

    Resin Jewelry Making (Bangles, rings, and earrings)

  8. 08

    Resin Souvenir & Customized Keychain Production (Customized keychains and souvenirs for weddings, birthdays, corporate anniversaries, graduations, burial ceremonies, and other occasions)

  9. 09

    Customized Resin Writing Pens (Creating refillable and non-refillable resin writing pens)

  10. 10

    Customized Jotters & Jotter Packages (Creating customized jotters and professionally presented jotter packages)

  11. 11

    Customized Phone Pouch Making

  12. 12

    Customized Resin Mirrors & Combs

  13. 13

    Customized Resin Hair Clips

  14. 14

    Resin Wall Clock Making

  15. 15

    Resin Photo Frame
