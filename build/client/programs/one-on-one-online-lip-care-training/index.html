KPX - 1 ON 1 LIVE LIP CARE PRODUCT FORMULATION TRAINING

Develop practical lip care formulation skills through personalised one-on-one online training. Receive individual guidance on formulating lip gloss, lip oils, lip scrubs, lip balms and lip creams, with practical instruction designed to help you understand the formulation process and improve your production skills.

Online training2 days

About this programme

KPX Academy's One-on-One Live Lip Care Formulation Training offers personalized online instruction for individuals who want to develop practical skills in creating different lip care products. Through live video sessions, you will receive step-by-step guidance on formulating different lip gloss varieties, lip scrubs, lip creams, and lip balm. You will also learn essential formulation techniques, colour mixing, ingredient selection, safety precautions, and wholesale production. The training introduces product branding and packaging, pricing, marketing, and business development to help you understand how to turn your lip care formulation skills into a profitable business. As part of the training package, you will receive a PDF containing 40 bonus lip care formulation recipes, lifetime access to mentorship, and supplier contact information. Upon successful completion of the training, you will receive a Certificate of Completion.
