KPX - CHINA IMPORTATION TRAINING

Learn how to import products from China to Nigeria through our practical China importation training. Discover how to source products from platforms such as 1688 and Pinduoduo, navigate supplier communication, make payments, arrange shipping and calculate importation costs, with practical guidance designed to help you understand the importation process.

Online trainingSelf-paced /Flexible

About this programme

Learn the fundamentals of China-to-Nigeria importation through a practical, beginner-friendly training program. You’ll learn how to source products from platforms such as 1688 and Pinduoduo, identify reliable suppliers, make payments, work with sourcing and repacking agents, understand air and sea shipping, calculate your landed costs, and develop effective importation strategies. The training is designed for beginners, online vendors, business owners, and anyone interested in starting or improving an importation business.

Learning outline

What the programme covers

Explore the topics included in this programme.

  1. 01

    Introduction to China Importation Business (Why importation is popular in Nigeria, local market buying vs. direct importation, types of importation)

  2. 02

    China Sourcing Platforms (Introduction to sourcing platforms, 1688, Pinduoduo, platform differences)

  3. 03

    Product Sourcing & Supplier Verification (How to search for products effectively, identifying reliable suppliers, questions to ask suppliers, avoiding fake suppliers and scams, requesting product videos and verification)

  4. 04

    Payment Methods & Account Setup (Understanding payment methods, how to open and set up Alipay and WeChat, how to fund your Alipay or WeChat account, using Nigerian bank cards for payment)

  5. 05

    Sourcing & Repacking Agents (Understanding sourcing agents, choosing reliable agents, understanding repacking agents)

  6. 06

    Shipping from China to Nigeria (Air shipping, sea shipping, shipping companies, choosing reliable shipping companies)

  7. 07

    Product Weight & Shipping Cost Calculation (Understanding product weight, air shipping calculations, sea shipping calculations, CBM)

  8. 08

    Landing Cost & Profit Calculation (Calculating landed cost, spreading shipping costs across products, pricing and profit calculation)

  9. 09

    Documentation & Record Keeping (Importation records, documentation and proper cost tracking)

  10. 10

    Importation Business Strategies (Pre-order business strategy, common beginner mistakes, practical strategies for building a sustainable importation business)

  11. 11

    Guidance & Support (Final importation tips, questions, support and discussion group access)
