KPX - PHYSICAL LIP CARE PRODUCT FORMULATION TRAINING

Join our hands-on physical lip care formulation training and develop practical skills in producing different lip care products. Learn how to formulate lip gloss, lip oils, lip scrubs, lip balms and lip creams through guided practical sessions, with instruction covering product formulation, production techniques and preparation for commercial production.

In-person training3 days

About this programme

KPX Academy's Physical Lip Care Formulation Training is designed to equip participants with practical skills in creating different lip care products through in-person demonstrations and hands-on practice. The training covers lip gloss, lip scrubs, lip creams, and lip balm, alongside essential formulation techniques, colour mixing, ingredient selection, safety precautions, and common formulation mistakes. Participants will also learn how to make the most of their ingredients, create different colour variations without purchasing every colour available, and develop their products for retail or wholesale. Beyond formulation, the training introduces product branding, packaging, pricing, marketing, and business development to help participants understand how to turn their skills into a business. Participants also receive a lip care formulation recipe booklet for future reference and lifetime access to mentorship.
