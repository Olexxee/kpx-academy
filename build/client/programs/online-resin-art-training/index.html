KPX - ONLINE RESIN ART & CRAFT TRAINING

Learn resin art and craft skills through our online training in Nigeria. Explore how to create resin jewellery, keyholders, bookmarks, bangles, rings, signage and other customised resin crafts through practical video lessons, detailed guidance and structured training designed to help you develop your creative skills.

Online trainingSelf-paced/Flexible

About this programme

Learn the fundamentals of resin art and how to create a wide range of beautiful, customized and sellable resin products. This online training is designed for beginners and is conducted exclusively on Telegram. IMPORTANT: This online training takes place exclusively on Telegram. No other platform is used for the class.
