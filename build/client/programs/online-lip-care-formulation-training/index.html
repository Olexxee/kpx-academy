KPX - ONLINE LIP CARE PRODUCT FORMULATION TRAINING

Learn practical lip care formulation skills through our online training in Nigeria. Explore the formulation of lip gloss, lip oil, lip scrubs, lip balms and lip creams through structured training designed to help you develop practical production skills.

Online trainingSelf-paced/Flexible

About this programme

Learn how to formulate a variety of lip care products through a practical, beginner-friendly online training program. The course covers essential materials and tools, product formulation, coloring and finishing techniques, basic lip care concerns, wholesale production, and product customizations. The training is delivered exclusively on Telegram and is structured to allow participants to learn at their convenience.

Learning outline

What the programme covers

Explore the topics included in this programme.

  1. 01

    Introduction to Lip Care Formulation (Basic materials and tools, product handling and preparation)

  2. 02

    Lip Gloss Formulation (Clear lip gloss, peach lip gloss, strawberry lip gloss, pink pigment lip gloss, dazzling red lip gloss, butterfly holographic lip gloss, glittered lip gloss, bubblegum lip gloss, chocolate lip gloss, raspberry lip gloss, nude lip gloss, color-changing lip gloss)

  3. 03

    Lip Scrub Formulation (Lemon lip scrub, strawberry lip scrub, mint lip scrub)

  4. 04

    Lip Cream & Lip Balm Formulation (Pink lip cream, large-batch pink lip cream for wholesale production, lip balm/chapstick)

  5. 05

    Wholesale Lip Care Production (How to produce lip care products for wholesale, large-batch production)

  6. 06

    Basic Lip Care Concerns (Understanding basic lip care concerns and product considerations)

  7. 07

    Lip Tube Printing & Customization (How to print on lip care tubes and customize product packaging)

  8. 08

    Bonus Recipes & Practical Videos (40 bonus lip care recipes with detailed practical videos)

  9. 09

    Supplier Sourcing & Mentorship (Supplier contacts and lifetime mentorship access)
