KPX – 1 ON 1 LIVE RESIN ART TRAINING

Get personalised online resin art training through one-on-one practical guidance and instruction. Learn resin art techniques, material preparation, resin mixing, colour application and the creation of different resin crafts through individual online sessions designed around your learning pace and practical development.

Online training1 -2 weeks

About this programme

Learn resin art through personalized, one-on-one live video training with direct guidance throughout your learning process. The training covers resin preparation, measuring ratios, color mixing, resin pouring, embedding techniques, and the creation of customized resin crafts, accessories, and souvenirs. This option is suitable for learners who prefer individual instruction or cannot attend physical training in Ile-Ife. You can interact with the trainer, ask questions, and receive guidance during the live video sessions. You will also receive practical business guidance and lifetime mentorship access.
